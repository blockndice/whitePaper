challenge mechanic web2 → web3 if success
Challenger
Sets a challenge

Chooses a specific objective. Stakes a reward. Waits for someone to take it.

vs
Attacker
Tries to win

Any player can attempt to complete the challenge within the allotted time.

✓ Goal reached

The Attacker receives the reward. Verified automatically.

✗ Not reached

The Challenger gets their stake back in full. Challenge unbeaten.
